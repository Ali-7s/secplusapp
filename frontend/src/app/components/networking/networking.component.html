Why this section exists

Security+ assumes you already know how networks work (CompTIA recommends the Network+ course first), and many exam questions only make sense once the basics click. This path starts from nothing at all: what a network is and what travels across it. It then builds up, one idea at a time, to the parts of networking the exam leans on.

  • Start with lesson 1 and do them in order the first time. Each lesson builds on the ones before it.
  • No word is used before it is explained. Each lesson opens with a table of its new words in plain English, and a reminder of what it builds on.
  • Each lesson ends with a short check so you know whether it landed.
  • Every device and protocol lesson ends with how to secure it. Locking things down like this is called hardening.
  • Come back to a lesson whenever a flashcard does not make sense. Cards link to their lesson, and the cards at the end of each lesson show their definition when you hover over them.
